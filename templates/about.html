About Smart Traffic AI

Revolutionizing traffic management with artificial intelligence and computer vision

Project Overview

This web application demonstrates an AI-powered, density-based traffic signal control system. It leverages state-of-the-art YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection models to identify vehicles in traffic intersection images, automatically counts lane-wise density by dividing the scene into quadrants, and proposes intelligent green time suggestions based on vehicle distribution.

The system is designed to reduce traffic congestion, improve flow efficiency, and provide data-driven insights for traffic management decisions. By analyzing real-time or captured traffic images, the system can help optimize signal timing and reduce waiting times at intersections.

How It Works

1

Image Upload

Users upload a traffic intersection image through the web interface.

2

Vehicle Detection

YOLO model processes the image and detects all vehicles with bounding boxes.

3

Quadrant Division

The image is divided into four quadrants (lanes) based on the center point.

4

Density Calculation

Vehicles are counted per quadrant to determine lane-wise density.

5

Signal Timing

Green time is calculated proportionally: time ≈ vehicles / 5 seconds

6

Visualization

Annotated image with lane divisions, vehicle detections, and winning lane highlight.

Important Disclaimer

The signal timing rule used in this demonstration (green time ≈ vehicles / 5 seconds) is a simplified demonstration logic designed for educational and proof-of-concept purposes.

Real-world deployments require:

  • Calibration with traffic engineering standards and regulations
  • Integration with real-time video feeds and traffic sensors
  • Consideration of pedestrian crossings, emergency vehicles, and special events
  • Extensive testing and validation in controlled environments
  • Compliance with local traffic management policies
